Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) - scheme summary

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY was launched on 23 September 2018. It is a health assurance scheme for poor and vulnerable families and is considered one of the largest government funded health insurance schemes in the world. It is implemented by the National Health Authority.

The scheme provides a cover of 5 lakh rupees per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. There is no restriction on family size or age of family members. Treatment is cashless and paperless at public hospitals and at private hospitals that are empanelled under the scheme. The cover includes pre-hospitalisation and post-hospitalisation expenses, and many pre-existing conditions are covered from the first day.

Beneficiary families were identified using the deprivation and occupation criteria of the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 for rural and urban areas. A beneficiary can check eligibility on the official PM-JAY website or by calling the helpline, and can get an Ayushman card at an empanelled hospital or a common service centre by showing identity documents.

Ayushman Bharat also includes Health and Wellness Centres that provide primary care. Information in this document is a general summary and is not medical advice.
