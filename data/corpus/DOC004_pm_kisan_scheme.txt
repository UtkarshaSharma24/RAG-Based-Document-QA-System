Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) - scheme summary

PM-KISAN was launched in February 2019 by the Government of India. It is run by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare and is fully funded by the Central Government. The scheme gives income support to farmer families so that they can meet farming and household needs.

Under the scheme, eligible farmer families receive 6,000 rupees per year. The amount is paid in three equal instalments of 2,000 rupees each, one instalment every four months. The money is sent directly to the bank account of the beneficiary through Direct Benefit Transfer, so no middleman is involved.

At the start, the scheme covered only small and marginal farmer families who owned up to two hectares of land. In June 2019 it was extended to all farmer families irrespective of the size of their land holding.

Some categories are not eligible. These include institutional landholders, and farmer families in which a member holds or has held a constitutional post, is a serving or retired government officer (except multi-tasking staff and Group D employees), pays income tax, or is a registered professional such as a doctor, engineer or lawyer. Farmers must register on the PM-KISAN portal with land records and Aadhaar-linked bank details, and complete e-KYC to keep receiving instalments.
